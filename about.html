Our Story

From the workshop floor to your home

Stackly Artisan began with a simple belief: that the objects around us should carry the warmth of the people who made them. In an age of machines and mass production, we set out to build a premium marketplace where independent artisans are seen, celebrated, and fairly rewarded.

Today, we work with potters, weavers, woodcarvers and craftspeople across India — curating pieces that honour centuries-old techniques while feeling at home in modern lives. Every product is verified handmade, fairly priced, and shipped with the care it deserves.

What We Stand For

Craft, people and planet in balance

Three pillars guide every decision we make as a marketplace.

Empowering Makers

Fair pricing, clear payments and a global audience that lets artisans thrive doing what they love.

Sustainable Choices

Natural dyes, reclaimed timber and low-waste making. Every piece is designed to be kept, not replaced.

Timeless Craft

We champion heritage techniques that deserve to survive, pairing them with design that feels effortlessly modern.

Sustainability

Kind to the planet, by design

Handmade is inherently sustainable — but we go further. Our artisans use natural pigments, ethically sourced materials and reclaimed timber wherever possible. We ship plastic-free with minimal, compostable packaging and offset the carbon of every delivery.

  • 100% natural and reclaimed materials where possible
  • Plastic-free, compostable packaging on every order
  • Carbon-offset shipping across all deliveries
